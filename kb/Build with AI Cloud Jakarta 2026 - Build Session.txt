## **Introduction**

Welcome to the Build Session! This is your space to experiment, build, and push the boundaries of what's possible using **Antigravity IDE**. Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting out, this session is designed to be hands-on, collaborative, and most importantly, creative.

## **Logistics**

- Build Time: 14:45 - 16:20
- Format: **Team up (max. 5 people)** with other participants!
- Showcase: Starting at 16:20, we will hold a rapid-fire showcase
  - Length: **3 minutes**
  - Format: One Slide pitch deck (you can use the [templates here](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/15HJzsFnp0x3x-2pQzBSNXkW3lCGZ1tzcO2-nFXmHGPQ/edit?usp=sharing), or create your own!) and your working demo powered by **Antigravity IDE**

## **Possible Starter Topics**

- **Developer Experience** -

ex: Streamlining workflows, reducing friction, and improving the day-to-day life of engineers

- **Personal Productivity** -

ex: AI-assisted task management, focus tools, and smarter daily planning

- **Health & Wellness** -

ex: Habit tracking, mental health check-ins, and personalized wellness coaching

- **Learning & Education** -

ex: Adaptive tutoring, skill-gap analysis, and on-demand knowledge tools

- **Creative Projects** -

ex: AI co-creation for music, writing, design, and art exploration

- **Financial Wellness** -

ex: Budgeting, spending insights, and financial goal-setting made accessible

- **Team Collaboration** -

ex: Async communication, meeting summaries, and shared context across teams

- **Career Growth** -

ex: Resume tools, interview prep, mentorship matching, and skill development

- **Home & Lifestyle** -

ex: Smart home management, meal planning, and everyday decision support

- **Travel & Discovery** -

ex: Trip planning, local exploration, and culturally immersive experiences

- **Anything! Make a truly unique solution**
